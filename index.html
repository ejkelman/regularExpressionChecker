How it works

The expression is parsed, then converted to a position (Glushkov) automaton with one state per symbol. The automaton is paired with itself, and the regex is ambiguous exactly when two different states can be reached on the same word and both can still finish in accepting states. A star over something that can match the empty word is always ambiguous.
